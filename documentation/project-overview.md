\# Project Overview



\## GasFlow



GasFlow is a desktop-based Gas Agency Management System that was created and designed for one of my clients to simplify and automate gas agency operations.



The system centralizes inventory management, customer records, billing, payments, cylinder movement tracking, reporting, and backup into a single application.



\## Problem Statement



Many gas agencies rely on manual registers, spreadsheets, and paper records to track cylinders and customers.



This often results in:



\- Duplicate records

\- Billing errors

\- Lost transaction history

\- Difficulty tracking cylinder movement

\- Reporting challenges

\- Data loss risks



\## Solution



GasFlow provides a structured digital workflow that allows agencies to manage their operations efficiently while maintaining accurate records and business insights.



\## Goals



\- Reduce manual work

\- Improve inventory visibility

\- Simplify billing

\- Improve customer management

\- Generate reports quickly

\- Protect business data through backups
